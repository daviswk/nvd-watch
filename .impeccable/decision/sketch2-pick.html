Re-check rule for Not Scheduled CVEs when attack signals rise

As posted November 7, 2026 · Weekly scorecard of the NIST NVD and CISA KEV
Copy citationDownload brief
22.6%of CVEs published in the last 30 days carry CPE

A published re-check rule would re-queue roughly 250–470 CVEs a month, about 5–9 percent of NIST's enrichment capacity, at a crude cost of $270,000 to $510,000 a year. The Commerce OIG identified about $400,000 a year of re-scoring effort NIST could redirect. The rule needs no new appropriation.
