Why this matters for policy

NVD moved quickly on PHP2Shell, but only because it was high-profile. The KEV signal that now drives NVD's enrichment priority lagged industry telemetry by at least a day. For lower-profile CVEs that never make headlines or KEV, the scanner panel may stay at "Received" indefinitely.
